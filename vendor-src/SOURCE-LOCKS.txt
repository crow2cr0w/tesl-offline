These directories hold the pinned source snapshots and upstream license files.
The five tools/runtime-embed/*.dll files are generated at build time by
./tools/build-runtime-deps.sh and are deliberately not distributed in source.
The builder requires Mono mcs and the Roslyn compiler bundled with a .NET SDK.
